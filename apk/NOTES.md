## Alkhamis Launcher 3.2.2

**English**
- New name: Alkhamis Launcher.
- Arabic home: the app row scrolls and the focused app stays fully visible with any number of apps; the next row is no longer clipped.
- Remote navigation through the top bar (Settings, Wi-Fi, Inputs) now works in Arabic and English.
- Deleting built-in apps: the "Allow debugging" popup appears only once (tick Always allow), every step shows a clear result, a Test connection button was added, and protected apps offer Disable instead.
- Arabic uses Western digits unless you choose Arabic-Indic in Language.

Installs as an update over earlier versions. Downloader code: 4430541 (always the latest).
