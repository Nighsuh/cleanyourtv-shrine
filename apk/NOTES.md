## Alkhamis Launcher 3.2.6

**English**
- Dock: the selected app always stays fully on screen with the highlight on it, including the last app in Arabic. The row no longer jumps up on the 7th app, and the rounded band no longer leaves a cut-off curve.
- Holding OK on any dock app shows remove, hide and uninstall.
- The debugging popup scrolls on 1080p, so every button can be reached.
- Missing Arabic text has been added (debugging buttons, wallpaper and more). The screensaver's next-prayer line follows the language. Date spacing is fixed in Arabic.
- Up and Down no longer get stuck in time-zone search, the pairing fields, the wallpaper address or the dim slider. Long settings rows wrap instead of clipping.

Installs as an update over earlier versions. Downloader code: 4430541 (always the latest).

**العربية**
- شريط التطبيقات: التطبيق المحدد يبقى ظاهراً بالكامل مع الإطار عليه، حتى آخر تطبيق بالعربي.
- الضغط المطوّل على أي تطبيق في الشريط يعرض الإزالة والإخفاء وإلغاء التثبيت.
- نافذة تصحيح الأخطاء أصبحت قابلة للتمرير.
- استكمال الترجمة العربية وإصلاح التنقل بالأسهم في الإعدادات.
